Catering & Food Trailer Booking Questions

Can we hire your food trailer for an event?

Yes. Arizona Sunrise offers on-site food trailer service for school events, employee appreciation meals, HOA gatherings, festivals, and private celebrations. Send us your date, location, and estimated guest count to discuss availability and a menu.

Do you offer catering without the trailer?

Yes. We offer pickup and drop-off catering for meetings, parties, and family gatherings. Service details, timing, and pickup arrangements are confirmed with your order.

How do I request a catering or food trailer quote?

Use our catering inquiry page, call 945-546-0808, or email hello@azsunrisecafecito.com . Include your date, event location, guest count, and any menu or dietary preferences. We’ll confirm availability and discuss your options.
