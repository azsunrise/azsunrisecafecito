Meet Jack

Jack has spent decades in professional kitchens — from Arizona resorts to restaurant ownership and catering.

Jack has a gift in the kitchen — everything he touches just tastes better. The first time he cooked Wendy a burger in his backyard, it tasted like steak. That was the moment she thought, “Well… I’m going to marry this man.”

He’ll tell you it’s just good ingredients and technique. We’ll tell you it’s talent.

After stepping away to raise their twins, Jack returned to the kitchen with renewed purpose — bringing experience, instinct, and heart into every dish.

Meet Wendy

While Jack leads the kitchen, Wendy leads the business side — overseeing operations, planning, and customer communication. She’s a natural mentor and team-builder who brings clarity and steady leadership to every moving part.

Wendy has always led by serving others — consistently, and with a high bar for doing things the right way. She connects the dots quickly — between people, ideas, and details — creating alignment and momentum wherever she’s needed. You’ll feel it in how smoothly everything runs.

You’ll also find her working alongside Jack in the kitchen, supporting prep and ensuring each service runs with care and consistency.

Catering & Food Trailer Bookings

From smaller gatherings to workplace meals and community events, we offer pickup and drop-off catering, along with on-site food trailer service by arrangement. As a hands-on, two-person team, we work with you to plan a menu and service that fit your event, with quality, freshness, and personal attention at the center.

Service is scheduled by booking. Pickup locations and times are confirmed with each order. Share your event date, location, guest count, and menu ideas so we can discuss availability and a quote.
